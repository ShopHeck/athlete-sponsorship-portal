How it works

From a few phone photos
to a sold-out kit.

    Send your photos

FAQ

Questions athletes ask.

How does the 3D model look so much like me?

We generate it from your guided photos, then you approve it inside your private portal preview. If it isn't right, you can rebuild before anything goes live.

Who pays the card fees, refunds and chargebacks?

Sponsors pay invoices on your own Stripe account. Stripe's processing fees come out of those payments, and refunds and chargebacks are handled on your account. Our fee is taken automatically from each payment.

Do sponsors need an account?

No. Sponsors pick a placement, enter their company and email, and bid or lock it. Winners receive a Stripe invoice by email.

My promotion has sponsor rules. Is that a problem?

No. You choose which placements are offered and confirm final artwork personally before anything is printed or shown.

Can I put the portal on my own website?

Yes. Every portal has a one-line embed code, and its own link to share on social media.

What sports does it work for?

We're starting with combat sports — bare knuckle, MMA, boxing, Muay Thai and kickboxing, and jiu-jitsu and grappling — because kit placements are a proven sponsorship format there. Apply if you compete in something else; we'd like to hear from you.
