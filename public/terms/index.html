These terms apply to everyone who uses Athlete Sponsorship Portal (“the platform”, “we”, “us”): athletes who publish a sponsorship portal (“athletes”) and businesses that bid on or buy a placement (“sponsors”). By using the platform you agree to them.

1. What the platform does

The platform hosts interactive sponsorship portals where athletes offer logo placements on their apparel and sponsors can bid on or buy them. We provide the software. Each sponsorship agreement is between the athlete and the sponsor.

2. Bids and Lock It Now

  • A bid is a binding offer to sponsor the placement at the bid amount if it is the highest bid when bidding closes.
  • “Lock It Now” is a binding offer to buy the placement immediately at the listed price.
  • Nothing is charged on the portal. Winning sponsors receive an invoice by email, payable through Stripe.
  • An athlete may decline a sponsor or artwork, for example where a promotion, athletic commission or venue rule does not allow it. If a paid placement can't be used for that reason, the athlete arranges a refund.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. For athletes using their own Stripe account, invoices are issued and paid on that account; Stripe's fees, refunds, disputes and chargebacks are handled on the athlete's account under Stripe's terms. Our platform fee is deducted automatically from each payment at the rate agreed with the athlete. We never store card details.

4. Sponsor artwork

Sponsors confirm they own or are licensed to use the logos they upload, and grant the athlete and us permission to display them on the portal and, once a placement is won or bought, on the athlete's apparel and promotional material for the event. Final artwork is confirmed with the athlete before printing.

5. Athlete likeness and 3D models

Athletes confirm they have the right to use the photos they upload and consent to us generating a 3D model of their likeness for their portal. Models stay private until the athlete approves them and we publish them. An athlete can ask us to take their model offline at any time.
