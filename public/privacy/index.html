Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Athlete Sponsorship Portal (“we”, “us”) collects, why, and who it's shared with.

What we collect

  • Sponsors: company name, contact name, email, optional phone number, bid amounts and any logo you upload.
  • Athletes: name, email, portal content, Stripe account status, and — if you use the 3D model studio — the photos you upload and the 3D model generated from them.
  • Applicants: the details you submit in the founding athlete application.
  • Technical data: basic request logs (such as IP address and browser) kept by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

How we use it

To run sponsorship portals and auctions, send bid, invoice and sign-in emails, build and display approved 3D models, respond to applications, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. We don't sell personal information.

Who we share it with

  • The athlete whose portal you bid on receives your bid and contact details so they can arrange the sponsorship.
  • Stripe processes invoices and payments.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Netlify hosts the platform and stores its data.
  • Meshy processes athlete photos to generate 3D models, only when the athlete uses the model studio.

Sponsor names and logos are shown publicly on a portal once a placement is won or bought.

How long we keep it

Bid and payment records are kept as long as needed for the event, accounting and dispute purposes. Athlete photos and models are deleted on request when they're no longer needed for a live portal.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing sponsors@michaelheckert.com. Some records may need to be kept where the law requires it.

Changes

We'll update the date above when this policy changes.
